# Hunter quadruped footsteps V5

Ten seconds, eight separate paw contacts: two four-beat walking cycles.
Order: left hind, left fore, right hind, right fore. Each paw lands separately;
there are no simultaneous diagonal pairs or running/galloping impacts.

The foreclaws carry the deeper slam, longer scrape and stronger hull vibration.
The hindclaws have a shorter, firmer contact and shorter scratch. Left/right
material pitch differs subtly. Contact gaps remain 1.11-1.38 seconds, with a
repeating four-phase rhythm rather than a uniform two-beat march.

The listening preview remains mono, 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3. Matching PCM16
WAV master and separate foreclaw/hindclaw WAVs are included for later use.
No game integration, spatial panning or enemy movement code was changed.

This is recorded-steel editing plus original synthesized low-frequency weight,
not a live recording of a creature. Same CC0 sources and licenses as
[V4 source notes](hunter-massive-steel-v4-sources.md). Previous versions remain
unchanged.
