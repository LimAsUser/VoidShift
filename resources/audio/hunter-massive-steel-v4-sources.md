# Hunter massive steel footsteps V4

Eight-second, six-step composite. Approximately 1.25 seconds between footsteps.
Recorded metal is edited, cropped, pitch-lowered, filtered, enveloped and layered
with original synthesized short sub impacts and structural vibration. This is
not an AI audio generation or an unedited creature recording.

## Source recordings

The source pages identify all three recordings as Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
permitting modification and commercial use. Checked on 2026-09-28.
Only the publicly embedded HQ MP3 previews were downloaded; no authenticated
original-download endpoint was used. Final WAV files are lossless masters of
the composite, not lossless copies of the original field recordings.

- **Metal Scrape** by **magnuswaker**. Large metal panels scraping against an ATX
  panel; source of the layered claw drag.
  https://freesound.org/people/magnuswaker/sounds/530075/
  Preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/530/530075_11537497-hq.mp3
- **Heavy Metal Impact** by **magnuswaker**. Recorded bed-frame impact, processed
  by its author; source of the short hard-claw contact.
  https://freesound.org/people/magnuswaker/sounds/614062/
  Preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/614/614062_11537497-hq.mp3
- **Closing large metal door-003** by **AudioPapkin**. Field-recorded metal-door
  material; source of the shortened, lowered deck impact and quiet after-rattle.
  https://freesound.org/people/AudioPapkin/sounds/814881/
  Preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/814/814881_8698658-hq.mp3
